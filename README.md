Hello this is the most generic desktop streamer for your CFW equipped Wii U Gamepad


Screen Sharing support is very experimental and may crash your gamepad needing a full restart to work again, you can use it as a game controller if you deactivate "Stream Screen"


This Program is vibecoded by codex, the ideas came out by me :D

Extract the Windows x64 ZIP or install the exe file, open WUP Stream on the GamePad, and enter its IP in the app. Supports screen and audio streaming, touch as mouse, Xbox 360 controller input and vibration, brightness, and microphone capture to WAV or optionally VB-CABLE. Camera, NFC and infrared require receiver support.

Requires WUP Stream on the GamePad, ViGEmBus and .NET Framework 4.8. Tested with a simulated GamePad.
